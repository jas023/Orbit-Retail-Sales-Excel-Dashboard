# 🛍️ Orbit Retail & Services – Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Orbit Retail & Services Sales Performance Dashboard** is an interactive data analytics project designed to analyze retail sales performance across products, regions, and time periods.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs), sales trends, product-level analysis, regional comparisons, and profitability insights.

The objective is to transform raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights that support better decision-making, sales optimization, and strategic planning.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability performance.
- Track monthly sales trends and compare performance between 2023 and 2024.
- Identify top-performing products based on sales and profit.
- Evaluate regional sales contributions and profitability.
- Understand the relationship between product quantities and net sales.
- Analyze discount patterns across products and regions.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks five major business performance indicators:

- **Net Sales:** ₹795.42 Million
- **Total Profit:** ₹119.37 Million
- **Total Orders:** 4,500
- **Loss-Making Orders:** 0
- **Average Profit Margin:** 15.12%

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Net Sales Trend
- Compares monthly sales performance between 2023 and 2024.
- Highlights seasonal fluctuations and year-over-year growth.

### 2. Top 5 Products by Sales
- Identifies the highest revenue-generating products.
- Provides a quick comparison of product-level sales contributions.

### 3. Top 5 Products by Profit
- Highlights products generating the highest profits.
- Helps identify key contributors to overall profitability.

### 4. Product-wise Quantity vs Net Sales
- Compares product quantities with their corresponding net sales.
- Identifies products with strong sales value and demand.

### 5. Region-wise Net Sales vs Profit
- Evaluates sales and profit performance across five regions.
- Helps compare regional contributions to business performance.

### 6. Average Discount % by Products & Region
- Analyzes average discount percentages across product categories and regions.
- Helps understand discount distribution and pricing patterns.

### 7. Insights & Recommendations
A dedicated insights panel highlights important business observations and suggested actions, making the dashboard more useful for business decision-making.
